Welcome To My Dream Jop

My dream jop is to be an computer science teacher for kids with disabilities.

Why I Choose This Jop?

I choose this path because I believe children with disabilities deserve the same chance to learn, create, and dream as any other child. Computer science allows me to turn challenges into possibilities by using technology as a bridge to communication independence, and self-confidence. Teaching them is deeply meaningful to me because every small achievement proves that ability is not defined by disability.
